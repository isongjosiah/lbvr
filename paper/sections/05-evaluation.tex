\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:eval}

\subsection{Setup}
\label{sec:eval:setup}

We evaluate on a Synthea FHIR R4 corpus of 100\,000 living patients (114\,693 bundles, 430.25\,GiB; P50 2.23\,MB, P99 37.9\,MB). Experiments seed deterministically; each run records an environment fingerprint. Tiers are simulated with calibrated lognormal latency distributions tracking Pinata, Filebase, and Arweave/Irys (P50/P99: 80/400\,ms hot, 120/600\,ms warm, 500/8000\,ms cold). Gas measurements use Foundry's in-memory EVM at the Cancun fork, opcode-equivalent to Cardona; live-Cardona spot-check is journal scope.

\subsection{Time-to-availability and PoR cost}
\label{sec:eval:tta-por}

Time-to-availability post-PUT (E4, sim-mode), $n=100$, log-cadence polling 100\,ms~$\to$~5\,min: hot reaches 99\% reachability within 510\,ms, warm within 2\,s, cold within 120\,s; zero polling-budget timeouts. Cold-tier tail is the chain-settlement signature; clinical workflows are bounded by the hot+warm path.

PoR proof cost (Figure~\ref{fig:e5}) measures responder prove time and verify gas across Merkle proof depths $\{3,5,7,9,11,13\}$ (128\,KiB--128\,MiB). Prove time is constant at $\approx 630\,\mu$s P50: BLS sign dominates, Merkle proof contributes \(< 2\,\mu\)s. Gas for \texttt{respondToChallenge} grows linearly (290\,k at depth 3, 533\,k at depth 13); \texttt{postChallenge}/\texttt{recordVerdict} are depth-independent at 122\,k/75\,k.

\subsection{Byzantine withstand}
\label{sec:eval:byzantine}

Against the uniform adversary (Figure~\ref{fig:e6}), retrieval success degrades smoothly across $f \in \{0, 0.1, 0.33, 0.5, 0.67\}$: 100\%~$\to$~67\%~$\to$~48\%~$\to$~19\%~$\to$~16\% (500 retrievals/fraction). \texttt{recovery\_failed} is zero throughout: the visible failures are decrypt errors on optimistic fast-path commits, not RS reconstruction failures.

The tier-selective adversary (Figure~\ref{fig:e6b}) is qualitatively different. At $f \le 0.33$ the adversary is invisible (100\% success) because the fast path uses hot+warm only and the targeted cold tier is engaged solely on reconstruction. The cliff is sharp: 50\% at $f=0.5$, 0\% at $f=0.67$. PoR cadence does not flag the adversary since it serves correctly on challenges. This detection gap is the new measurement; the journal extension adds per-tier PoR-vs-retrieval divergence monitoring at the gateway.

\subsection{Erasure recovery}
\label{sec:eval:erasure}

Figure~\ref{fig:e9} reports single-tier failure recovery (D0/D1/P0 missing) over 50 bundles $\times$ 5 reps. D0 or D1 recovery is bounded by cold-tier wait; P0 missing is a fast-path no-op. Figure~\ref{fig:e9multi} extends to double failures: the early-fail short-circuit (Section~\ref{sec:protocol:retrieval}) drops D0+D1 detection to 137\,ms median (P99 559\,ms)---without it, the gateway waits the full cold-tier P99 ($\approx 8$\,s). Parity-included pairs (D0+P0, D1+P0) wait the cold-tier context before committing recovery: 562\,ms median, 8.7\,s P99.

\subsection{Storage cost, end-to-end SLO compliance, and cross-SLO calibration}
\label{sec:eval:slo}

Storage cost (E7, analytical) at the 100\,K-corpus scale (430.25\,GiB) is \$50.55/mo for LBVR-Med's RS(2,3) layout, 5.1$\times$ cheaper than naive 3$\times$ Pinata replication (\$258/mo) and within 1.7$\times$ of S3 3-region replication (\$29.69/mo) while delivering decentralized governance, on-chain provenance, and Byzantine withstand the latter lacks.

The end-to-end SLO attainment synthesis (E8) composes Sections~\ref{sec:eval:ingest}--\ref{sec:eval:tta-por}: IEC~60601-1-8 alarm (10\,s) is PASS with margin; clinical radiology open (2\,s) is STRETCH under WAN adversity; chart-pull (500\,ms) FAILS at 5\% loss and lands at the SLO boundary at baseline. The composite per SLO ignores ingest (decouplable via pre-staging) and rolls up retrieval-baseline + stress + TTA via strictest-wins.

Cross-SLO calibration (E10, post-processed from E2) places the LBVR-Med retrieval distribution against five domain SLO thresholds spanning four orders of magnitude. Power-grid GOOSE (4\,ms, IEC~61850) is 0.0\% feasible---no decentralized verifiable storage configuration meets a sub-10\,ms budget when even AES-GCM open + Merkle re-verify costs $\sim$5\,ms on commodity x86. The clinical and scientific-DT regimes (radiology open 2\,s and the interTwin notional 5\,s budget) are met at 100\%. The chart-pull 500\,ms boundary sits at 98.2\% feasibility; this is the limiting clinical workflow and the load-bearing finding for Section~\ref{sec:discussion}.
