\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Safety-critical federated systems such as clinical decision support, scientific digital twins, and power-grid digital twins need a storage substrate that is simultaneously decentralized (no single operator can withhold or alter data), latency-bounded inside a domain SLO (retrieval completes before the workflow times out), and verifiable (a third party can confirm what was retained, by whom, and when). No deployable substrate satisfies all three today: centralized cloud concentrates trust, while commercial decentralized substrates exhibit unbounded settling times and produce no signed provenance---Trautwein \emph{et al.}~\cite{trautwein2024ipfs} measure public IPFS PUTs at ``dozens of seconds to minutes.'' Recent work chips at individual properties: Fang \emph{et al.}~\cite{fang2024metaverse} pair proof-of-retrievability with vector commitments on a single homogeneous pool; interTwin~\cite{manzi2025intertwin} federates five backends but reports neither recovery latency nor PoR cadence; the TRL-3 review of Li \emph{et al.}~\cite{li2025trl} confirms that across dozens of proposals, none publish P99, recovery, or PoR numbers on realistic workloads. Yet EHDS~\cite{ehds2025reg}, EU AI Act Art.\ 12~\cite{aiact2024}, and HIPAA NPRM~\cite{hipaanprm2025} make all three properties non-negotiable for healthcare deployment.

We present Pure-LBVR, a verifiable storage fabric for safety-critical federated systems, instantiated on healthcare as the regulatory motivating case; the architecture is domain-neutral. We make four contributions:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Cross-tier erasure-coded redundancy.} RS(2,3) across three decentralized backends with measured single- and double-tier recovery at 100K-patient scale.
  \item \textbf{Cryptographically-attested provenance.} An additive W3C PROV-JSON~\cite{w3cprov} extension with BLS-signed retrieval receipts, hash-anchored via JCS canonicalisation~\cite{rfc8785}.
  \item \textbf{Cross-SLO calibration.} First P99 measurement for decentralized verifiable storage against clinical (IEC~60601-1-8~\cite{iec60601-1-8}), scientific-DT, and power-grid (IEC~61850 GOOSE~\cite{iec61850goose}) regimes.
  \item \textbf{Tier-aware Byzantine withstand.} Resilience under uniform and tier-selective adversaries, surfacing a detection gap that PoR challenges miss.
\end{itemize}

Section~\ref{sec:related} positions Pure-LBVR against prior work; Section~\ref{sec:method} presents the system, threat model, and protocol; Section~\ref{sec:eval} reports 13 experiments at 100K-patient scale; Section~\ref{sec:discussion} maps results to regulatory obligations and outlines extensions.
